Privacy Policy

Friday · Last updated 31 August 2026

Friday is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant operated by a single individual for their own use. It is not a commercial service and has no users other than its owner. This policy describes how it handles data.

Who this covers

Friday has exactly one user: its owner and developer, reachable at litsydkamal@gmail.com. No accounts are offered to anyone else and no other person's Google data is accessed.

What Google data Friday accesses

  • Google Calendar — to read, create, and update events on the owner's own calendars, so it can answer scheduling questions and manage the owner's day.
  • Gmail (send only) — to send email that the owner has asked it to send. Friday does not request permission to read, search, or delete the owner's mailbox.

Where the data goes

  • Calendar and message data is processed on a Raspberry Pi located in the owner's home.
  • Conversation text and relevant context are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for language processing, under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere else. There is no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, and no third-party data broker.

What is stored, and for how long

Friday keeps a local memory consisting of a SQLite database and markdown files on the device in the owner's home. These hold conversation history, notes, and an audit log of actions taken. The owner can inspect or delete these files at any time. Nothing is stored on a server operated by anyone else.

Data sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, rented, traded, or shared with any third party, other than the processing described above. It is not used to build advertising profiles, train models, or serve any purpose beyond providing the assistant features the owner asked for.

Limited Use. Friday's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on the local device and are not committed to any code repository or shared with anyone. Access is tiered: certain actions require explicit confirmation from the owner before Friday performs them, and every action is written to an audit log.

Revoking access

The owner may revoke Friday's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately ends Friday's ability to read or write any Google data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
